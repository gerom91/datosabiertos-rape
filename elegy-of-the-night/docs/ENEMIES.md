# Elegy of the Night — bestiary & bosses

All enemies are **new designs** (original names and art). Stats are balanced for the expected player
level in each area. Damage to the player = `max(1, ATK − DEF/2)`; player DEF ≈ 13 at the start,
≈ 30 mid-game, ≈ 70 late. Player HP ≈ 90 → 450. Player ATK ≈ 20 → 100.
Give every enemy a short bilingual bestiary `desc` (EN + ES), 1–2 sentences, flavourful.

Already implemented (examples, `js/data_enemies.js`): `ghoul`, `night_bat`, `bone_scribe`, `warg`.

## Bosses → `js/data_bosses.js`
Arena geometry is built by the room agents exactly as described. All bosses: `boss:true`, big HP bar.
Give each boss at least 4 distinct, telegraphed attacks, a phase change around 50 % HP, hit flash,
screen shake on heavy impacts, particles, light sources, death explosion sequence (`onDeath` can spawn
extra explosions over ~2 s with `g.later`). Fair but challenging for the expected level.
